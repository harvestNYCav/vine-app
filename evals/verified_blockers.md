# Verified blockers

Every blocker was checked by its own verifier: 36 fresh subagents, plus one more for lesson 05 quiz item 9, which is currently a major.
- Each verifier saw only the lesson file path, the on-screen section name, the quoted text and the claimed problem. None saw the evaluator's tags or reasoning.
- Verifiers could read the lesson file, `lib/lesson-answers.ts` for grading claims, and the web for current facts. None wrote any file.
- No lesson file was changed.
- **Applied afterwards:** these verdicts were later added to `evals/lesson_*.json` and `summary.md`.
  - B04 was rejected and moved to `rejected_findings`.
  - B17, B22 and B36 were overruled and stay blockers, because an activity counts as required unless the lesson marks it optional.
  - That same rule raised 2 more role-plays to blocker: lesson 06 Part 2 and lesson 12 Part 3.
  - **Final count: 37 blockers.**

The verifiers applied these rules:
- **Blocker:** factually wrong English or a wrong grammar rule; a wrong or overly narrow answer key in the auto-graded student app; a required or graded question asking learners to disclose family, immigration, legal or trauma details, or a real address or phone number.
- Optional activities are never blockers.

## The two items you asked about

- **Lesson 09, Tutor slides › Guided practice: Describe Someone You Know:** **confirmed**. The guided-practice activity has no optional marker and no alternative: its instructions tell learners to describe a family member, and its scripted prompts ask 'What does your brother like?' and 'What does your sister like?', so it is a required family disclosure that also assumes they have siblings.
- **Lesson 05, Tutor slides › In-person quiz, item 9 (iq9). Currently a major, not a blocker.:** **downgrade-to-major**. The iq9 model answer 'I like ___-ing.' leaves out 'I like to ___', which the lesson itself says is equally correct, so a volunteer could mark a right answer wrong; but it is a tutor-run in-person item whose English is accurate and which says 'Answers will vary', not an auto-graded student-app key, so it is not a blocker.

## Verifier disagreements on the disclosure rule

The verifiers did not agree on when an ungraded tutor activity counts as "required":
- **Confirmed** as required disclosures, because the activity isn't marked optional and offers no made-up alternative: B09 (06 guided practice), B11 (07 guided practice), B13 (09 guided practice) and B35 (18 role-play Part 1).
- **Downgraded** because they are ungraded or tutor-led: B17 (10 guided practice), B22 (12 role-play Part 1) and B36 (18 role-play Part 3).
- **B22 and B35** are very similar role-play prompts, both asking learners about their own family, yet they got opposite verdicts.
- **The fix is the same either way:** add a made-up-person option. If you settle the rule (for example, "an ungraded tutor activity is required unless it says it is optional"), I can apply it consistently.
